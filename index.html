Text Message Opt-In & Consent

This page explains how customers opt in to receive SMS messages from AXL SOLUTIONS.

Who is sending messages?

Messages are sent by AXL SOLUTIONS to provide real-time updates related to Flight Landing Notifications.

Contact: axl.mhai@gmail.com • Phone: +1 (866) 551-8969

Use-Case: Airline Landing Notification Project

This SMS program sends time-sensitive, transactional flight arrival updates to travelers and airport pickup/ground coordination teams. Customers opt in to receive automated alerts based on live flight status events.

  • Flight ETA updates and delay notifications
  • Landing confirmation
  • Gate / terminal changes (when available)
  • Baggage claim carousel assignment (when available)
  • Pickup or shuttle coordination instructions

This service does not send marketing or promotional texts. Messages are only sent to users who explicitly opt in.

What you’ll receive

  • Flight ETA / delay alerts
  • Landing confirmation updates
  • Gate, terminal, or baggage claim changes (if applicable)
  • Pickup coordination instructions
  • Customer support responses

Message frequency: 2–6 messages per trip, depending on flight events and assigned pickups.

How to opt in (Via Text)

Customers opt in by sending a keyword to our toll-free number.

Step 1: Text JOIN to +1 (866) 551-8969
Step 2: You will receive a confirmation message asking you to reply YES to subscribe.
Step 3: After replying YES, you are subscribed and may receive messages as described above.

We only send messages to customers who explicitly request SMS communication.

Example messages (what SMS will look like)

These are sample texts customers may receive:

AXL SOLUTIONS: Thanks for subscribing to Flight Landing Alerts. You’ll receive ETA/landing updates for your trip. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help. Msg & data rates may apply.
AXL SOLUTIONS: Flight {FlightNumber} to {Destination} is now estimated to arrive at {ETA}. We’ll notify you when it lands. Reply STOP to opt out.
AXL SOLUTIONS: ✅ Flight {FlightNumber} has LANDED at {AirportCode}. Gate: {Gate} • Terminal: {Terminal}. Reply STOP to opt out.
AXL SOLUTIONS: Baggage claim for Flight {FlightNumber} is Carousel {CarouselNumber}. Reply HELP for support. Reply STOP to opt out.
AXL SOLUTIONS: Gate change for Flight {FlightNumber}. New Gate: {NewGate}. Check airport displays for updates. Reply STOP to opt out.

STOP / HELP / Opt-out

  • Reply STOP to unsubscribe at any time.
  • Reply HELP for support.

After you text STOP, you will no longer receive messages unless you opt in again.
